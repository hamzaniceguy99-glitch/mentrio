How we work

A mentor, not a course

You can already watch a hundred hours of startup advice for free. None of it looks at your funnel, your pricing or the email you're afraid to send.

Mentrio is one person working on your specific business, every week. We'll tell you when the idea needs to change and when you just need to make twenty more calls. We don't take equity and we don't promise outcomes.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Do I need a registered business first?

No. Plenty of clients start while validating an idea. Once you take money, we'll tell you to talk to an accountant and an attorney about structure — that part isn't our lane.

Do you take equity or finder's fees?

No. You pay a monthly fee and that's the whole relationship. We don't invest, broker paid introductions, or take a percentage.

Will you help me raise money?

We'll help you sharpen the story and the numbers you present. We don't introduce investors for compensation and we can't promise a raise.

What if my business is offline?

That's fine. Local service businesses, studios and shops all fit these sprints. The channels change, the method doesn't.

How much time does it take?

Between 5 and 10 hours a week depending on the sprint. The sessions are the easy part; the actions between them are the work.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

What's actually blocking the business?

Twenty-five minutes to find out, and an honest view on whether mentoring helps. Free.
